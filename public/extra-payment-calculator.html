Why a little extra goes a long way

Early in a mortgage, most of each payment is interest. Every extra dollar applied to principal skips all the future interest that dollar would have generated — so even a modest monthly amount, paid consistently, can cut years off the loan. Make sure extra payments are applied to principal, and confirm your loan has no prepayment penalty (most don't). If cash flow is tight, a refinance or recasting might fit better — happy to talk it through.
