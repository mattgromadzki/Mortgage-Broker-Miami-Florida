Lower payment, longer tail

Consolidating works because mortgage rates are usually far below credit-card rates, and the balance is spread over a long term — so the monthly number drops, sometimes dramatically. The tradeoff is time: a credit card you might have cleared in a few years becomes part of a 30-year loan, which can mean more total interest unless you keep paying extra. Use the savings to get ahead, not just to free up room to borrow again. We'll model your exact situation honestly before you commit.
