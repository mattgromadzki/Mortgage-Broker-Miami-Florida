What this chart is — and isn't

The 30- and 15-year lines are the real Freddie Mac weekly national averages: conventional, conforming loans for strong-credit borrowers putting 20% down. They're a benchmark for the direction of the market, not a quote. The FHA and VA tiles are estimates positioned relative to the conventional average — Freddie Mac's free feed doesn't publish FHA, VA, or jumbo averages, so those would need a dedicated rate-data provider to show as live figures. For your actual rate on any program, the scenario tool and a quick call are the real answer.
