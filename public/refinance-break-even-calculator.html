Reading the break-even

The break-even month is when your accumulated monthly savings finally exceed what you paid to refinance. Plan to stay past it and the refi pays off; sell or refinance again before it and it may not. Watch the term too — restarting a 30-year clock lowers the payment but can add interest over the full life, while a shorter term often saves interest at a higher payment.
