How your mortgage payment is built

Your monthly payment is more than principal and interest. Lenders usually collect property taxes and homeowners insurance with the payment and hold them in escrow, and if your down payment is under 20% on a conventional loan, private mortgage insurance (PMI) is added until you reach 20% equity. HOA dues, where they apply, are paid on top. Adjust any field above to see the breakdown update instantly.
