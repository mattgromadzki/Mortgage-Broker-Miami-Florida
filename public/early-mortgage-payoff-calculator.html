Set a date, hit the number

Most payoff calculators ask how much extra you'll pay; this one starts from the finish line. Choose the year you want to own the home free and clear, and it solves for the payment that gets you there, the extra above your current payment, and the interest you'd avoid. If the required payment feels steep, slide the goal out a few years — even shaving five years off a 30-year loan saves a meaningful amount of interest.
