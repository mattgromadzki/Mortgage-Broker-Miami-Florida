How affordability really works

Lenders look at two ratios: your housing payment versus income (front-end, often around 28%) and your total monthly debt versus income (back-end, commonly up to 43%, sometimes higher). The lower of the two limits sets your ceiling. Paying down a car loan or credit card can raise your buying power more than a small rate change — and a real pre-approval confirms the number before you shop.
