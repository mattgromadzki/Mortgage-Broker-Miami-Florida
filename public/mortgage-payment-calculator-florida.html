Why Florida payments surprise people

In much of Florida, homeowners insurance and HOA or CDD fees move the monthly payment more than a small rate change does. Insurance has risen sharply, condo and newer-community fees can be substantial, and CDD assessments fund community infrastructure in many developments. Pricing the payment with realistic Florida numbers up front avoids surprises later — call if you want help pinning down the real figures for a specific home.
