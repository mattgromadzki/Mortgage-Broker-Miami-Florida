FAQ

Common questions

What does a mortgage broker actually do?
A broker works with many lenders instead of just one bank, so we can shop your scenario across multiple options to find a better rate or program fit — then manage the process for you from application to closing.
What credit score do I need?
It depends on the program. FHA can work with lower scores, while conventional and jumbo prefer stronger credit. Even if a bank already said no, there's often a path — let's talk through yours.
Can you help with Miami condos?
Yes — condos are a specialty. I handle warrantable and non-warrantable buildings, pre-construction, and the building-review questions that trip up other lenders.
I'm self-employed. Can I still qualify?
Absolutely. Bank-statement and other alternative-income programs let business owners and 1099 earners qualify without traditional tax-return-only underwriting.
How fast can I get pre-approved?
Often the same day once I have your basic information. A pre-approval letter shows sellers you're serious and tells you your real budget.
